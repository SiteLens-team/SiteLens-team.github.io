Privacy Policy

For SiteLens — Website & SEO Analyzer (the “Extension”).

SiteLens analyzes the webpage you are viewing entirely inside your browser. We do not operate a backend for the Extension, we do not create accounts, we do not upload page content, and we do not use artificial intelligence or third-party LLM services. SiteLens collects nothing and sends nothing.

Preferences (language, appearance) and Site Audit results are stored locally on your device with chrome.storage.local. Exported reports are written to a location you choose.
